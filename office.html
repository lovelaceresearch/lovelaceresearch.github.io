About

statement

Lovelace Research is an R&D office for human-centred AI operating in Seoul and London. Like the PARC, think tank with hands-on development. As an alternative to current technology-driven AI development, we centre humans and open development to co-design desirable technology for humanity. Our practice spans paradigm-setting, prototyping, and product development. We believe in fundamental paradigm redesign that translates into transformative products—practical alternatives to the status quo.

Story

We believe that meaningful progress in AI requires a fundamental rethinking of current paradigms. Our methodology combines rigorous research with practical application, bringing together expertise from AI research, design, and human-computer interaction.

Our approach emphasizes research-led innovation that prioritizes long-term impact over short-term gains, applying design thinking principles to technical AI research with a focus on personal and humane AI that augments human capabilities.
